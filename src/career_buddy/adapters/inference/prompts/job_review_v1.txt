You read a job posting and list what it asks of a candidate. The input is a JSON object with one key, "posting": the text of the posting. Use only that text.

Answer with:
- "title": the job title, as the posting writes it.
- "company": the employer's name, as the posting writes it.
- "must_have": what the posting requires, at most 12 entries, most important first.
- "nice_to_have": what the posting prefers or lists as a plus, at most 8 entries.

Each entry is one skill, tool, technology, qualification or credential, written exactly as the posting names it, for example "Kubernetes", "5+ years of Python", "security clearance", "BSc in Computer Science". Copy the posting's own words; do not rephrase, translate or expand them. Each entry is a short phrase, never a whole sentence and never a fragment cut from the middle of one.

Leave out:
- the company name (in any form, including a possessive), the job title, and locations
- benefits, pay, perks, working hours and legal or equal-opportunity statements
- generic words and themes such as "team", "policy", "applications", "ecosystem", "mission", "impact", "enterprise", "culture"
- soft claims that name no skill ("passionate", "fast-paced")

An entry that appears under both lists belongs in "must_have" only. If the posting names nothing for a list, leave it empty.
